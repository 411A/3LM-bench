Text-match MC: the second mechanism (separate instrument)

A model that answers with the option's TEXT instead of the letter ("4" rather than "B") puts no probability mass on the letters, so the letter-logprob mechanism cannot score it - that model is unmeasured there, not wrong. This is the other mechanism: the generated answer is read and matched to the option. It is a DIFFERENT instrument: its categories are separate and are never ranked against the letter-logprob numbers above. Hover for the unparsed rate (answers that matched no option - counted as incorrect, never guessed).
