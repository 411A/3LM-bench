Methods and interpretation

Comparisons: use like-for-like metrics only; do not compare ranks across different instruments. The radar is limited to binary accuracy/clean-rate suites. Scores are task-level, not a universal measure of model quality. Tables retain exact values and intervals.

Multiple choice: letter-logprob scores use single-token answer-letter probabilities. Text-match scores read the generated answer and match it to an option; this distinct mechanism is not directly comparable. Unparsed answers count as incorrect, never guessed.

Standard s30: stratified 30-item official-split samples, not full-leaderboard results. MMLU-6, HellaSwag, and WinoGrande had upstream answer-position imbalance; gold positions were permuted deterministically with questions and distractors unchanged. Constant-picker accuracy is retained in the source data.

Qualifier: identical fast subsets for screening only; b11558 qualifier runs are not compared with b11714 screening results. CS-100: 100-item decider; paired McNemar results are shown below.

Agentic: pass^k requires ordered tool use; trace rate measures valid tool traces; answer-only ignores tool use. Summary values use each model's deepest run, so chain counts differ; deep-dive shows 19-chain evidence where available and otherwise the screening subset. Broken tool-call backends remain visible but are not comparable. Speed: isolated throughput uses one model on an otherwise idle machine, after two warmups and across 12 prompts. Historical in-run rates were recorded under background load and are not speed claims.

Missingness: — means not measured, never zero. 0% is an observed score. Incomplete runs are marked and are not silently treated as complete.
